# 🖐️ Finger Tracking and Drawing

A real-time **finger tracking and air drawing** web app powered by **MediaPipe Hands** and **vanilla JavaScript**.  
The project lets you draw in the air using your webcam — your finger becomes the brush!

## 🚀 Features

- ✋ Tracks your hand in real time using AI (MediaPipe Hands)
- 🎨 Draw on screen just by moving your index finger
- 🧼 Clear button to erase the drawing
- 📱 Works on both desktop and mobile browsers
- 🔒 Uses secure webcam access (HTTPS)
- ⚡ Lightweight — only HTML, CSS, and vanilla JS

---

## 🧠 How It Works

1. The app requests access to your webcam.  
2. MediaPipe detects your hand and identifies landmarks (like fingertips).  
3. The index finger’s position is tracked and used as a digital pen.  
4. As you move your finger, the app draws smooth lines in real time.  
5. You can press the “Clear” button anytime to erase the canvas.

## ⚙️ Usage

1. Open the app in your browser (make sure it’s served via **HTTPS**).  
2. Allow access to your webcam when prompted.  
3. Show your **index finger** to the camera and start drawing in the air!  
4. Press **Clear Drawing** to erase your canvas.  

💡 Tip: Use good lighting and a clear background for better hand detection.

---

## 🧼 Clear Button

Clicking the **Clear Drawing** button instantly resets the canvas, removing all previously drawn lines.  
If the button doesn’t work, make sure the camera is active and your browser has permission to use it.

---

## 🧪 Troubleshooting

- **Permission Denied:**  
  Ensure the site is served over HTTPS or localhost — browsers block webcam access otherwise.

- **No Hand Detected:**  
  Check lighting conditions and hold your hand within the camera frame.

- **Mobile Users:**  
  Use your **front camera** for a better experience.
